About Me

Hi, I'm Edrel P. Aquino. I am a Computer Science student and aspiring developer, driven by a genuine curiosity for how complex systems operate. I describe myself as a "tinkerer" at heart, someone who isn't satisfied with just using technology, but needs to understand the mechanics underneath

My technical journey is defined by a hands-on approach. I daily drive Arch Linux, not just for the challenge, but for the granular control it offers over my environment. Whether I am writing Java or C applications or backtesting automated trading strategies, I am constantly looking for ways to optimize workflows and solve logic puzzles. I enjoy the intersection of code and finance, exploring how technical analysis and algorithms can interpret market movements.

When I’m away from the terminal, I focus on the simple, tangible things: tweaking handheld gaming devices, tracking my fitness progress, or cooking up efficient, hearty meals. I value authenticity and clarity, both in the code I write and the life I lead.
